Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, handle and disclose user-related data for Spiral Ball Fun. Whenever you download, install, launch or interact with this game application, you confirm you have fully read, comprehended, and accept all data-handling practices laid out within this document. If you disagree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue using our Service. We hold the right to adjust policy terms as business workflows or local regulatory requirements evolve, and we encourage users to regularly check the privacy page inside App Settings for updates.

1. Information We Collect

The scope of collected data depends on your feature usage, permission selections and account status. We strictly follow data-minimization principles and only gather information required to keep the spiral ball puzzle running smoothly, stabilize system performance and improve overall player experience. Main data categories are listed below:

•                      Device-Related Technical Metadata: This set of data covers SDK version identifier, distribution channel marker, public IP address, operating system release version, App build number, advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), device hardware model, manufacturer brand, session start-stop timestamps, system language preference, time-zone configuration, and active network type (Wi-Fi / cellular network). Partial runtime hardware metrics may also be gathered, including memory occupancy, GPU load and storage usage status, for solving frame-drop and stalling issues.

•                      In-Game Behavioral & Puzzle Interaction Records: We collect anonymous gameplay logs generated during spiral-ball levels, including stage attempt counts, spiral-track passing speed, failure retry frequency, hint-item consumption volume, unlock status of ball skins, challenge mode participation, and user interactions with rewarded advertisements and bonus missions. Aggregated analysis over these records helps us fine-tune spiral-track difficulty gradients, roll out fresh puzzle chapters and fix gameplay logic defects.

•                      Voluntarily-Submitted Account Profile Data: Should you choose to register or log-in through supported third-party authorization channels including Google Sign-In and Apple ID, we obtain your authorized email address, public display alias and profile avatar. This information is solely used to initialize your player account, complete identity verification, sync spiral-ball puzzle archives, skin collections and achievement records across multiple mobile devices. We will never fetch extra private information irrelevant to account creation.

2. How We Utilize Collected Information

All obtained data shall only be processed for lawful service-oriented purposes. We will never divert your data toward unstated or unrelated commercial activities without your separate explicit consent. Core usage scenarios include:

•                      Service delivery and technical optimization: Analyze puzzle-session statistics to identify performance bottlenecks, repair runtime bugs such as level-progress loss or scene loading failures, and adapt game rendering logic for low-end and high-end mobile hardware.

•                      Advertising and reward-mission delivery: Cooperate with qualified ad service vendors to display suitable in-app advertisements and watch-for-reward tasks, leveraging device advertising identifiers. We make reasonable efforts to ensure ad content does not interrupt core spiral-ball puzzle gameplay.

•                      Cross-device account maintenance: For logged-in users, synchronize completed spiral levels, unlocked ball appearances, accumulated in-game bonuses and achievement milestones so you can resume your puzzle experience on different devices.

•                      User communication and support processing: Deliver in-app notifications regarding newly-released spiral chapters, limited-time skin events, important bug-fix announcements and policy revisions. We also rely on your registered email to reply to gameplay feedback, fault reports and user service tickets.

3. Circumstances of Information Sharing

We will not sell, rent or trade your personally identifiable information to external parties for marketing purposes without your clear authorization. Information may only be shared under the following limited compliance-aligned conditions:

•                      Authorized third-party service vendors: We may transmit necessary datasets to our trusted partners including ad mediation platforms, cloud-data hosting operators and user-behavior analytics agencies. These contractors can only access data required for completing assigned tasks and are bound by formal data-protection contractual clauses.

•                      Legal and regulatory obligations: We are permitted to disclose user information when responding to valid court orders, law-enforcement investigation demands or official regulatory audits. In such cases, we will only provide the minimal dataset required to satisfy legal duties and will notify you unless local law prohibits notification.

•                      Enterprise-level business transitions: If a merger, asset acquisition, enterprise reorganization or bankruptcy liquidation occurs, your stored user data may transfer as part of business assets to the successor entity. The successor must continue to follow this Privacy Policy’s data-protection standards, and we will issue user notifications via in-app pop-up or email when legally required.

4. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Where applicable under global data-protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA, we process your personal information based on the following lawful grounds:

•                      Contract performance: Processing necessary for delivering the game service you use, such as creating player accounts, synchronizing spiral-ball puzzle progress and running in-game reward mechanisms.

•                      Compliance with statutory obligations: Processing required to satisfy anti-fraud auditing, record-keeping and other legal compliance requirements.

•                      Legitimate interests: Processing carried out under balanced legitimate-interest assessment, for example securing App operation, detecting cheating behaviours, troubleshooting technical failures and optimizing puzzle content, provided such interests do not override your fundamental privacy rights.

•                      User consent: Applied for scenarios including personalized advertising rendering and optional feature activation. You retain the right to withdraw your consent at any time, which will not affect the legality of processing performed prior to withdrawal.

Please note: Basic spiral-ball puzzle gameplay remains available even if you decline to provide certain optional data; however account-related cross-device sync functions may become unavailable.

Your collected information may be transmitted to affiliated institutions or contracted service providers located outside your home jurisdiction. These overseas regions may adopt different local data-protection legal frameworks. To guarantee consistent data-protection standards during cross-border transmission, we implement corresponding safeguard measures, such as deploying TLS encrypted transmission channels, signing data-processing agreements containing Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with overseas partners, and complying with requirements set forth by CCPA, PIPEDA and other regional privacy statutes.

6. Your Personal Data Subject Rights

Subject to restrictions defined by applicable local laws, you enjoy the following rights in relation to your personal information:

•                      Access and correction: Request us to provide a copy of the personal data we hold for you, and demand revision if stored information is inaccurate or incomplete.

•                      Erasure and processing restriction: Submit requests for full account-data deletion, or require us to limit data-processing operations under qualified circumstances.

•                      Data portability: Obtain your personal account and gameplay-related data in machine-readable structured formats where technically feasible.

•                      Consent withdrawal: Revoke previously-granted consent for advertising or optional-feature processing by adjusting device privacy settings or contacting our support mailbox.

We only preserve personal data for periods necessary for service provision or legal compliance. Once data is no longer required, we will securely execute deletion or anonymization operations.

8. Data Security Safeguards

We adopt comprehensive industry-standard technical and administrative protection mechanisms to defend your user data against unauthorized access, tampering, leakage and loss:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile device and our backend servers is protected by TLS 1.3 encryption. Persistently-stored personal account archives adopt AES-256 encryption for static storage security.

•                      Strict need-to-know access-permission management is enforced: only authorized staff and contracted third-party personnel undertaking relevant business duties can access non-public personal information; all such individuals have executed binding non-disclosure agreements.

•                      We perform periodic security audits, vulnerability scanning and penetration testing for App client and server infrastructure, and keep updating security patches to counter emerging cybersecurity threats.

Please understand that no electronic-storage or internet-transmission technology can deliver absolute security. Although we adopt all commercially-reasonable protective countermeasures, we cannot fully eliminate data-security risks during network transmission.
